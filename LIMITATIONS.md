# Project Limitations
## Marketing Campaign & Conversion Analytics

## 1. Synthetic Dataset

This project uses synthetic educational data representing a fictional business.

The findings demonstrate analytical methods and business reporting capabilities. They must not be interpreted as actual company results or industry benchmarks.

## 2. Attribution Limitations

The dataset uses a last-touch attribution model.

Revenue is assigned according to the recorded attribution model. Other attribution approaches could produce different results.

Offline and view-through conversions are excluded.

## 3. Revenue and Profit Interpretation

ROAS represents attributed revenue divided by recorded advertising spend.

ROAS is not profit, ROI, or a complete measure of business profitability.

The difference between attributed revenue and advertising spend does not account for other operating costs.

## 4. Date and Period Limitations

The dataset covers January 2023 through December 2025.

December 2025 is partial and should not be directly compared with complete months without considering the difference in coverage.

Monthly leads are grouped by lead creation date, while conversions and revenue are grouped by conversion date.

Therefore, monthly conversion rates are not cohort-based and should not be interpreted as the conversion rate of leads generated in that same month.

## 5. Statistical Limitations

Statistical tests identify evidence of differences between observed distributions.

They do not establish that a marketing channel or campaign caused better performance.

Campaigns may differ in budget, objective, timing, and other characteristics.

Non-significant results do not prove that groups are identical.

## 6. Lead Score Limitations

Higher lead-score groups show higher observed conversion rates in this dataset.

This association does not establish causality or guarantee future conversion outcomes.

Lead scoring should be validated against new or out-of-sample data before being used as an operational decision rule.

## 7. Business Decision Limitations

Recommendations are based on the available synthetic dataset and its analytical results.

Before applying similar decisions in a real business, analysts should consider:

- Actual campaign costs and operating expenses.
- Customer lifetime value.
- Attribution quality.
- Campaign objectives and target audiences.
- Data completeness and tracking reliability.
- Additional business constraints.

## 8. Final Note

This project demonstrates an end-to-end marketing analytics workflow. Its purpose is to showcase data validation, KPI analysis, statistical reasoning, SQL reporting, Power BI visualization, and business-focused recommendations.

The results should be treated as educational analytical findings rather than evidence of real-world marketing effectiveness.
