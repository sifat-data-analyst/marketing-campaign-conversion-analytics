INTERPRETATION NOTES
- A small p-value indicates evidence of distribution differences, not proof that channel caused the difference.

- Campaigns may differ in budget, objective, dates, and other factors.
- ROAS is attributed revenue divided by spend; it is not profit or ROI.
- This dataset is synthetic; results must not be presented as real-company outcomes.
- Lead-source conversion percentages use whether a lead has any mapped conversion; they are not a causal attribution study.
